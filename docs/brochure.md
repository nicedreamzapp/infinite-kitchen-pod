## Page 4: customized to your needs

One 120 sq ft chassis, four factory configurations, same robot, your brand.

- **Glass curbside:** high visibility, QR ordering, sidewalk deployment
- **Solid secure:** for malls and nighttime; metal covers the windows, fully secure, no glass
- **Compact kiosk:** 1.8 m x 2.4 m for campuses and transit hubs, the smallest footprint
- **Gutted retrofit:** drop modules into a failed kitchen shell, 48 hour install, no construction

The configuration sheet also shows: a shared base chassis (2.4 m W x 3.0 m L x 2.6 m H) with roof-mounted HVAC and a solar-ready panel, leveling casters and suspension, an integrated 20 kWh LiFePO4 battery and power system (10 kWh on the compact kiosk; 10 to 30 kWh, solar ready, grid-tie option across the line), a modular utility bay (water tank, waste, electrical hub, data and control), swappable exterior panels (glass, stainless, composite, branded), plug-and-play modules (kitchen, cooling, POS, security), and compliance targets of IP66, NSF, fire safety, accessibility and remote telematics. The retrofit configuration lists 3-phase 240V/400V power. Reconciling these chassis dimensions and power figures with the 12 ft x 10 ft x 9.5 ft, 480V spec is tracked in [issue #8](https://github.com/nicedreamzapp/infinite-kitchen-pod/issues/8).
